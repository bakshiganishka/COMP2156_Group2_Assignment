George Brown College is one of the coolest colleges in Toronto. 
They have all kinds of programs like business, tech, health, and design. 
What’s great is that it’s super hands-on – you don’t just sit in lectures all day. 
You get to work in labs, do projects, and even get real-world experience through internships. 
Plus, they have lots of connections with companies in the city, so it’s easier to land jobs or co-op positions after you graduate.
